InverseBinomialMinimumTrials(Cumulative Probability, Probability of Success, Number of Successes)::
    Returns the minimum number _n_ of trials such that, for the given probability of success, the probability of obtaining at most the given number of successes is at most the given cumulative probability.

[EXAMPLE]
====
`InverseBinomialMinimumTrials(0.5, 0.2 ,50)` yields 254.
====
