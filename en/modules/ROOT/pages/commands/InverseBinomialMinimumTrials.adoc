InverseBinomialMinimumTrials(Cumulative Probability, Probability of Success, Number of Successes)::
  Returns the minimum number _n_ of trials where the probability of getting at most the given successes does not exceed the cumulative probability.

[EXAMPLE]
====
`InverseBinomialMinimumTrials(0.5, 0.2 ,50)` yields 254.
====
